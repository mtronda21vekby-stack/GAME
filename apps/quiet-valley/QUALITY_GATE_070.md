# Quiet Valley 0.7 visual acceptance gate

This release is accepted only from executed browser frames, not from shader/source claims.

- Preserve the restored 0.5 countryside density: cottage, bakery, windmill, market, pond, paths, crops, separate paddock and four regions.
- Mobile normal play must keep the central playfield clear; secondary information stays behind menus or contextual sheets.
- Desktop and mobile use the same scene and material implementation. Quality presets may change resolution/shadow/post cost, never replace the art.
- Day, rain/wet soil, water, foliage, contacts and evening lighting must remain depth-readable in Chromium and WebKit screenshots.
- Crop interaction, animal care, orders, production, save reload and four-region travel must still work.
- Physical iPhone visual/performance acceptance remains a separate manual gate.

Target: a strong commercial stylized browser-game presentation. This document does not claim photorealism or AAA certification.
