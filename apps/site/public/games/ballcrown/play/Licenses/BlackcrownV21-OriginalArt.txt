BLACKCROWN v21 original art and music fallback

Three new weapon designs (ROOK pistol, WASP Uzi, LANCER rifle), nine modular Sentinel parts and the compact reticle were authored as new SVG path geometry for this project. Original-game vector geometry or bitmap pixels were not used to construct these designs; source logical rectangles and placement matrices remain compatibility metadata.

Classic characters, original effects and other original-game assets retain their existing notices and provenance. This packet makes no ownership claim over those assets.

MP3 fallbacks encode the same already-authored ballcrown compositions from their existing WAV masters using the installed Unity-bundled LAME encoder. The encoder library is not copied or distributed. Original sound-effect credits remain unchanged.
