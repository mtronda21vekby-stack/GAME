Raze source and new local media notices

Raze © 2010 — Juice-Tin, AddisonR; original sponsor Armor Games.
The supplied original SWF and its preserved source snapshot remain unchanged.
Original Main.as context-menu attribution is retained in the source audit.
Original embedded font copyright fields and third-party notices remain there.
No separate original-game reuse licence was present in the supplied package.
This project and its delivery are private; no public deployment was performed.

The new native interface does not add the original advertising, branding intro
or external navigation destinations. Original game/creator attribution remains
visible in the native menus and pause interface and in this notice.

Interface typography uses Inter Regular and SemiBold under the SIL Open Font
License; the complete licence is in Licenses/Inter-OFL.txt. Original embedded
font programs are not redistributed by this browser project.

The browser corpse solver is translated from the recovered original AS3 Box2D
classes. Box2DFlashAS3 is an open source port of Erin Catto's Box2D library;
upstream project: https://box2dflash.sourceforge.net/ . The recovered compiled
bytecode does not contain the upstream source-comment licence headers. This
local translation retains its recovered game provenance; it does not claim
that a newer Box2D release is the original game's solver.
The upstream reference notice and altered-version identification are in
Licenses/Box2D-Upstream-Notice.txt.
The original game motion, collision, actor rigs and weapon/match definitions
remain distinct from new visual assets and interface code.

New menu/arena illustrations were created for this private redesign with
imagegen. Refined classic skins and weapon art preserve the original forms,
points of attachment and attribution; their SVG sources and 4096px masters
are kept in the single Unity project. Orbital and Vanguard are new authored
vector kits based on separate generated concepts. Their actual 4096×4096
PNG masters were rendered directly from the vectors, without raster upscaling
or adding empty borders. Runtime parts and local mod atlases are smaller.

Circuit Breaker, Zero Gravity and Final Vector are new synth compositions.
Their original source program and stereo WAV masters are in
Artwork/OriginalMusic; runtime AAC files are in Web/StreamingAssets/NewMusic.
They use no outside samples and do not adapt the original Raze melodies.
Original Raze sound-effect assets and their event names remain attributed
to the original game and are preserved alongside the new music.

AFTER YOU 0.14.0 and other personal files were not changed by this project.
